Each wearable

Apple Watch

Shortcut or app
Data
Activity (steps, workouts); sleep; heart (resting heart rate, heart rate, HRV); blood oxygen; other Apple Health readings.
New in 2026: a second HRV measure (RMSSD) with iOS 27 (not yet confirmed by Apple).
Data retrieval
Requires Apple Shortcut app or custom Caspia app
Notes
iPhone only. With the Shortcut, readings are sent when the user taps a link or on a weekly automation; with the Caspia app, they're sent on their own in the background. Health can't be read while the iPhone is locked, so a send can be skipped. Full history can also be exported once from the Health app and uploaded.

Garmin

Sign in once
Data
Activity (steps, workouts); sleep; heart (heart rate, HRV); stress and Body Battery; breathing and blood oxygen.
Data retrieval
Sign in with Garmin Connect once; standing connection
Notes
Works with any Garmin that syncs to the Garmin Connect app. No cost to the user.

Samsung Galaxy Watch

Custom app
Data
Activity (steps, exercise); sleep; heart rate; other Samsung Health readings.
New in 2026: irregular heart rhythm alerts and sleep apnea results.
Data retrieval
Requires custom Caspia app
Notes
Android only. The app sends new readings on its own in the background. The phone must be on, and the user sets the app's battery use to Unrestricted at setup so Samsung's battery saver doesn't pause it. The new 2026 data needs Samsung Health 6.30.2 or later. Without the app, the user can only text screenshots.

Fitbit and Google Pixel Watch

Sign in once
Data
Activity (steps, workouts); sleep; heart (heart rate, HRV); other Fitbit readings.
Data retrieval
Sign in with Google once; standing connection
Notes
Fitbit is now Google Health. Old Fitbit connections stop working Oct 30, 2026, so users connect through their Google account. Pixel Watch data syncs to the same account.

Oura

Sign in once
Data
Sleep, readiness and activity scores; heart (heart rate, HRV); other Oura readings, per type the user approves.
New in 2026: Gen 3 and Oura Ring 4 data can't be shared without an active membership.
Data retrieval
Sign in with Oura once; standing connection; needs an Oura membership
Notes
Membership is about $5.99 a month or $69.99 a year. Gen 2 rings connect without one. Without a membership, the user can still download their own files and upload them once.

Whoop

Sign in once
Data
Recovery, strain and sleep (by stage); workouts; body measurements. No continuous heart rate.
Data retrieval
Sign in with Whoop once; standing connection
Notes
The Whoop band only works with a Whoop membership.

Withings

Sign in once
Data
Body (weight, body composition); blood pressure; activity and workouts; sleep; heart (heart rate, ECG).
Data retrieval
Sign in with Withings once; standing connection
Notes
Covers Withings scales and blood pressure cuffs as well as watches.

Polar

Sign in once
Data
Workouts; daily activity; body details (weight, height). Current list not confirmed.
Data retrieval
Sign in with Polar Flow once; standing connection
Notes
Only data recorded after the user connects comes through, not earlier history.

Ultrahuman Ring

Sign in once
Data
Sleep; activity (movement, steps); heart (heart rate, HRV); temperature; VO2 max and recovery; glucose with the CGM add-on. No workouts.
Data retrieval
Sign in with Ultrahuman once; standing connection
Notes
Glucose only for users with Ultrahuman's CGM add-on. Workouts don't come through.

Pro-Patient Tech. Checked October 17, 2026. Wearable makers change what they share often; check the maker's own pages before relying on a detail. Items marked "not confirmed" come from reporting, not the maker.
